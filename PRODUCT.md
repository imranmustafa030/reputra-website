## Platform

web

## Users
Owners and managers of local businesses in Pakistan whose customers choose them on Google Maps: cafés and restaurants, salons, clinics (dental and skin first, as the sales beachhead), gyms, shops, workshops, hotels. Lahore first, then Karachi and Islamabad/Rawalpindi. They read on their phones (often budget Android on 4G), between customers or after closing. Their job: be the business people pick when they search "near me", which on Google Maps means more reviews and a higher rating than the competitor down the road.

## Product Purpose
Reputra is a review-growth system. It asks every happy customer for a Google review at the right moment, so the business's review count and rating climb, it rises on Google Maps, and more people call, visit and walk in. The one question every feature, page and report answers: how many new Google reviews did this business get because of Reputra?

Success for a business: a steadily rising Google review count and rating, shown before → after. Success for the site: an owner starts a free month by messaging on WhatsApp.

## Positioning
Reputra sells growth, not hardware. Customers reach one review page three ways: a counter stand they scan or tap, a tap card at the till, or a WhatsApp message sent one at a time from the business's own number. The page is in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English, opens without an app, and sends them straight to Google's own review box. Behind it, the owner gets the software: scans and clicks, a staff leaderboard, private feedback alerts, and a monthly report on WhatsApp.

Built for how Pakistan works: WhatsApp-first, Urdu-first, set up in person, priced in rupees, paid by Raast, JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer. Foreign tools (Birdeye, Podium) cost $300+/month in USD and are not built for WhatsApp or Urdu.

## Operating Context
- The customer pays and, on the way out, scans the QR or taps their phone on the stand at the counter (or on the card at the till). Or staff send a ready-made WhatsApp message with the link.
- The customer's flow: scan, tap or open the message → a review page with the business's logo and one question in their language → one button opens Google's review box. Private feedback is an extra option; the Google button is never hidden.
- The owner sees scans, Google clicks and WhatsApp requests in a dashboard, a staff leaderboard, and a monthly WhatsApp report with before → after growth.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Ways in: a counter stand (sturdy acrylic plate on a base, QR code and NFC tap on one face, the way JazzCash and Raast QR stands sit on Pakistani counters), an NFC tap card, and WhatsApp `wa.me` links from the business's own number. No bulk-sending tools.
- The stand and card are the one-time setup pack, not the product. The monthly plan is the software and support.
- Languages: Urdu, Roman Urdu, English.
- Planned prices (dummy until tested): Basic PKR 3,999, Pro PKR 7,999, Business PKR 14,999+ a month. Setup pack PKR 3,000–6,000 once (stands, card, setup in person). First month free. Yearly = two months free. Never priced per review.
- Rules Reputra follows: never hide the Google button from unhappy customers (no review gating), never offer gifts for reviews, never write or sell fake reviews, never use bulk WhatsApp tools, only message customers who visited.
- The primary action opens WhatsApp. There is no checkout or form backend yet.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Reputra (from "reputation"). Domain reputra.pk. Logo: a dot and one ripple arc (a tap and the signal it sends).
- The owner wants an award-level, premium, serious-company feel, with real motion and 3D-quality product presentation, never toy effects, fake device mockups or unrelated stock photos.
- The owner approved the current visual world on 26 Oct 2026: near-black stage, near-white sections, one amber accent, Geist, a procedural 3D card with engraved bands and a gold edge.

## Product Principles
1. Lead with the result: more reviews, a higher place on Google Maps, more customers. The stand, card and WhatsApp message are how, never what.
2. Honest before impressive: no invented proof. Trust comes from the person, the in-person setup and Google-policy honesty.
3. A busy owner on a phone must understand it in five seconds and act in one tap.
4. Speak the customer's language: Urdu and Roman Urdu are first-class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Budget Android phones on 4G are the norm, so the page stays fast and light. Urdu text renders right-to-left in Nastaliq. Reduced-motion users get the full content without movement.
