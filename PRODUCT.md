# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners and managers of dental and skin clinics in Lahore first, then Karachi and Islamabad/Rawalpindi. They read mostly on their phones (often budget Android on 4G), between patients or after clinic hours. Their job: get more Google reviews so their clinic wins the "dentist near me" / "skin clinic near me" search on Google Maps against competitors with far more reviews. Later audiences: salons, restaurants, gyms (not the focus of this site).

## Product Purpose
Reputra makes asking every patient for a Google review easy and automatic. Success for a clinic: a steadily rising Google review count and rating. Success for the site: a clinic owner starts a free month by messaging on WhatsApp.

## Positioning
Built for how Pakistani clinics actually work. Patients review from a QR stand on the reception counter, an NFC card they tap with their phone, or a WhatsApp message sent one at a time from the clinic's own number, in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English. It is set up in person, priced in rupees, and paid the way clinics already pay (Raast, JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer). Foreign tools (Birdeye, Podium) cost $300+/month in USD and are not built for WhatsApp or Urdu.

## Operating Context
- The patient pays at reception and sees the QR stand or NFC card on the way out. The receptionist may send a ready-made WhatsApp message instead.
- The patient's flow: scan or tap → a review page opens in the browser (no app, no login) with the clinic's logo and a question in their language → one button opens Google's own review box. Private feedback is optional, and the Google button is never hidden.
- The owner sees scans, Google clicks and WhatsApp requests in a dashboard. A staff leaderboard shows who brought in reviews. A monthly report arrives on WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Channels: acrylic QR stand, NFC tap card, WhatsApp `wa.me` links from the clinic's own number (no bulk-sending tools).
- Languages: Urdu, Roman Urdu, English.
- Planned plans (dummy until tested): Basic PKR 3,999, Pro PKR 7,999, Business PKR 14,999+ a month. Setup pack PKR 3,000–6,000 once. First month free.
- Rules Reputra follows: never hide the Google button from unhappy patients (no review gating), never offer gifts for reviews, never write or sell fake reviews.
- The primary action opens WhatsApp. There is no checkout or form backend yet.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Reputra (from "reputation"). Domain reputra.pk.
- The owner chose a white/light base, and the header with a live review ticker (later replaced, at the owner's request, by an honest offer header).
- The owner wants an award-level, premium, serious-company feel, with real motion and 3D-quality product presentation, not toy effects.
- The owner chose the "premium product film" hero direction: the product (phone, NFC card, stand) shown at high quality, with scroll-driven motion of the tap.

## Product Principles
1. Honest before impressive: no invented proof. Trust comes from the person, the in-person setup and Google-policy honesty.
2. The product is physical and local: a stand, a card and a WhatsApp message in a Lahore clinic. Show those things, not abstract SaaS.
3. A busy doctor on a phone must understand it in five seconds and act in one tap.
4. Speak the clinic's language: Urdu and Roman Urdu are first-class, not an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Budget Android phones on 4G are the norm, so the page stays fast and light. Urdu text renders right-to-left in Nastaliq. Reduced-motion users get the full content without movement.
